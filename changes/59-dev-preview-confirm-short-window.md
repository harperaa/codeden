- **The dev preview confirmation fits a short window.** The dialog "Run these commands on this computer?" is now never taller than the window: its text, the commands and the notes scroll inside it, and **Cancel** and **Save and allow** stay in view. Before, in a short browser window or a small desktop window, the dialog grew past the window's edge and the two buttons could not be reached with the mouse, most of all for a Secure Foundation project, whose notes make it taller.

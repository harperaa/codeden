- **Dev Servers: every dev preview in one place, to watch and to stop.** A new **Dev Servers** entry, directly under **Usage** in the sidebar, opens a page that lists the dev previews of all projects, the PM threads' and the sessions' alike: the project, what it belongs to (with a link to that PM or session page), its status and reason, its link, its ports, how long it has been up, and for a PM thread's preview the base branch and commit. The entry shows how many are running. **Stop** on a row, **Stop selected** for the rows you ticked, and **Stop all** (which asks first and says how many will stop) end the dev servers and free their ports. Each of them is the same as switching the preview off at the top of its own page, where the switch then shows off and switches it on again as before; that goes for the PM threads' previews after **Stop all** too, so nothing comes back by itself. **Stop all previews** in **Settings**, **Dev previews** is a different action and unchanged: it still leaves the PM threads' previews switched on, held until **Restart**. A stop that fails doesn't keep the others from stopping, and is named with its reason; **Settings**' **Stop all previews** now reports that too. The page and the strips follow each other without a refresh. Nothing on the page starts a preview: when nothing is running it explains how to switch one on and what a project needs first, with links to each project's PM page and Dev preview settings and two example pictures drawn from the app's own strip and settings section. Another device sees the list and can stop previews, as before, and can't start one or open a link.

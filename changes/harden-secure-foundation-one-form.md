- **Secure Foundation is one form with a Go button.** Pick the option, type a name, press **Go**: it checks the form, the folder, the template's commit, the provider and your sign-ins in one pass and, when all of it holds, creates the project and starts the setup. What used to be the Connections and Review steps is now one verification view, opened with **Verify Settings**; a Go that can't go on opens it on what stopped it, with what to do about each item, and creates nothing until you press Go again. Three lines above the buttons always say what will be created in your accounts, that the setup runs unattended, and that the app's URL only works while the setup session runs.
- **Secure Foundation installs every content module unless you choose otherwise.** "All modules" is now the preselected app shell (it was "Minimal"). Deploy stays off until you tick it, under **Advanced**.
